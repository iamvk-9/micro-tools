How to actually save a Reel

One honest note: a purely in-browser page cannot download Instagram Reels. Instagram keeps its video behind login and server-side logic that blocks browsers — no client-side tool (including this one) can bypass that. It is not a cop-out; it is how Instagram works, and our tools are built to be honest rather than pretend.

Your realistic options:

  • In-app save — In the Instagram app, tap the Share → Save video (bookmark icon) to add it to your saved collection. 100% reliable, respects the creator.
  • Screen record / live capture — Play the Reel fullscreen, then record your screen (built into iOS and Android). Works everywhere, keep it private.
  • Ask the creator — Send it to your device with the download or share sheet, or message the creator for a copy. Always respects rights & copyright.
  • Videos you own — If the Reel is yours, the original always exists in your Instagram account and you can download it from there.

Downloading content you don't own can violate Instagram's terms and the creator's rights. Use this tool for the legitimate options above — saving your own work or content you have permission for.
